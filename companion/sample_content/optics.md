title: Optics
---

Optics is the study of light and how it behaves. It examines how light travels, how it bends and reflects, and how it forms the images we see.

## Reflection and refraction

Light travels in straight lines until it meets a surface or a change of material. When it strikes a mirror it reflects; when it passes from one substance into another, such as from air into glass, it bends, an effect called refraction. These principles explain how lenses form images and how the eye works.

The study of light was greatly advanced by [Isaac Newton](isaac-newton.md), who showed that white light is a mixture of the colours of the spectrum. Earlier, [Johannes Kepler](johannes-kepler.md) had explained how lenses focus light.

## Instruments and light

Optics made possible the [Telescope](telescope.md), which gathers light to reveal distant objects, and the microscope, used by [Robert Hooke](robert-hooke.md) and [Antonie van Leeuwenhoek](antonie-van-leeuwenhoek.md) to explore the very small.

A deeper understanding came from [Electromagnetism](electromagnetism.md), which revealed that light is an electromagnetic wave. Optics thus links the everyday experience of seeing with fundamental ideas in [Physics](physics.md).

Optics remains vital to [Astronomy](astronomy.md), to medicine and to many technologies. From spectacles to great observatories, the control of light depends on the principles worked out through the patient study of this ancient and practical science.
