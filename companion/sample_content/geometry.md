title: Geometry
---

Geometry is the branch of mathematics concerned with shape, size, position and space. It is among the oldest fields of study and remains fundamental to mathematics and science.

## Foundations

Ancient mathematicians developed geometry as a system of careful reasoning, building conclusions from a small set of basic assumptions. This method of proof, in which each result follows logically from what came before, became a model for rigorous thinking throughout mathematics.

Geometry deals with points, lines, angles, surfaces and solids, and with the relationships between them.

## Uses and connections

Geometry has always been closely tied to [Astronomy](astronomy.md), since the paths of the [Planets](planets.md) and the shapes of their orbits are geometric problems. [Johannes Kepler](johannes-kepler.md) used geometry to describe the elliptical orbits of the planets.

The subject also feeds into [Calculus](calculus.md), which extends geometric ideas to curves and changing quantities. Mathematicians such as [Blaise Pascal](blaise-pascal.md) made notable contributions to geometry, studying figures and their properties.

Geometry is essential in [Physics](physics.md), engineering, architecture and navigation. It provides the tools to measure distances, describe motion and understand the structure of space. From the simple shapes of everyday life to the vast scales of the cosmos, geometry offers a precise language for describing the world, and it continues to be a living and expanding field of mathematics.
