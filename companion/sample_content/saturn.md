Saturn is the sixth planet from [The Sun](the-sun.md) and the second largest in the [Solar System](the-solar-system.md). It is best known for its magnificent system of rings, which make it one of the most beautiful sights in [Astronomy](astronomy.md).

## Rings and moons

Saturn is a giant planet made mostly of gas, similar in nature to [Jupiter](jupiter.md). Surrounding it is a broad set of rings made of countless pieces of ice and rock, ranging from tiny grains to large blocks. Saturn also has many moons, some of them large worlds in their own right.

Like the other [Planets](planets.md), Saturn follows an elliptical orbit around the Sun, in keeping with the laws found by [Johannes Kepler](johannes-kepler.md).

## Observation

When [Galileo Galilei](galileo-galilei.md) first observed Saturn through an early [Telescope](telescope.md), the rings puzzled him, since his instrument was not powerful enough to show them clearly. Later and better telescopes revealed their true nature.

Saturn's motion is governed by the [Theory of Gravity](theory-of-gravity.md) described by [Isaac Newton](isaac-newton.md), the same force that shapes its rings and holds its moons in place.

Saturn remains a favourite target for observers and a major subject of scientific study. Its rings offer a natural laboratory for understanding how such structures form and behave, and the planet as a whole helps scientists learn about the giant worlds of the outer Solar System.
