title: Palaeontology
---

Palaeontology is the study of ancient life through the evidence of [Fossils](fossils.md). It reveals the creatures and plants of the distant past and traces how life has changed over immense periods of time.

## What palaeontology studies

Palaeontologists examine the preserved remains of organisms that lived long ago, from tiny shells to the bones of great reptiles. By studying these fossils they reconstruct extinct animals and plants and work out how they lived. The subject depends closely on [Geology](geology.md), which helps to date the rocks in which fossils are found.

## Evidence for the history of life

Palaeontology provides some of the strongest evidence for the theory of [Evolution](evolution.md). The fossil record shows that many kinds of life once existed that are now extinct, and that living things have changed gradually over time, supporting the ideas of [Charles Darwin](charles-darwin.md).

The English collector [Mary Anning](mary-anning.md) made remarkable discoveries of ancient marine reptiles that greatly advanced the young science. Even earlier, [Robert Hooke](robert-hooke.md) had suggested that fossils were the remains of once-living creatures.

Palaeontology draws on [Zoology](zoology.md) and [Botany](botany.md) to understand the ancient forms it studies, comparing them with living species. It gives us a picture of a world that has changed enormously across deep time, from early simple life to the diverse creatures of later ages. It remains an exciting field that continues to reveal new chapters in the long history of life.
