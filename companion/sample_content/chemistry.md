title: Chemistry

Chemistry is the science of matter, its composition, its properties and the changes it undergoes. It studies the substances that make up the world and how they combine and react with one another.

## The elements and their combinations

At the heart of chemistry lies the idea that all matter is built from a limited number of basic substances called elements. These are organised in the [Periodic Table](periodic-table.md), which arranges them so that their patterns of behaviour become clear.

Elements join together to form compounds, and chemical reactions rearrange them to produce new substances. The forces that hold atoms together are electrical in nature, linking chemistry to [Electromagnetism](electromagnetism.md).

## Chemistry and the wider sciences

Chemistry is closely related to [Physics](physics.md), since both study matter and energy, and it draws on [Thermodynamics](thermodynamics.md) to understand which reactions occur and how much energy they release or absorb.

Great advances were made by many investigators. [Marie Curie](marie-curie.md) discovered new elements through her study of radioactivity, and [Michael Faraday](michael-faraday.md) uncovered laws governing chemical change brought about by electricity.

Chemistry is essential to biology as well, since the processes of life, including those studied in [Genetics](genetics.md), depend on chemical reactions within living cells.

Chemistry has enormous practical value, in medicine, industry, agriculture and everyday life. By revealing how substances are built and transformed, it provides a deep understanding of the material world and remains one of the central sciences.
