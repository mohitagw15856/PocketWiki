title: Gregor Mendel
---

Gregor Mendel (1822 to 1884) was a scientist and friar, working in what is now the Czech Republic, whose experiments with pea plants established the basic rules of inheritance. He is regarded as the founder of [Genetics](genetics.md).

## The pea plant experiments

Mendel grew and crossed thousands of pea plants, carefully recording traits such as seed colour and plant height across many generations. From these patient studies he found that characteristics are passed on in discrete units, later called genes, according to consistent rules.

He described how some traits are dominant and others recessive, and how they combine in predictable ratios. His use of large numbers and careful counting gave his conclusions unusual strength.

## Recognition

Mendel's work was little noticed during his lifetime, but it was rediscovered decades later and became the foundation of modern [Genetics](genetics.md). His findings fitted well with the theory of [Evolution](evolution.md) and [Natural Selection](natural-selection.md) proposed by [Charles Darwin](charles-darwin.md), together forming the basis of much of modern biology.

Mendel drew on the traditions of [Botany](botany.md) and careful observation in his monastery garden. A quiet and methodical man, he combined patience with mathematical insight. His experiments remain a model of clear scientific reasoning, and the laws of heredity he uncovered still bear his name.
