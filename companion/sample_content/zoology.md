title: Zoology
---

Zoology is the scientific study of animals. It examines their structure, behaviour, classification and the ways in which they live and interact with their surroundings.

## Scope of zoology

Zoologists study animals of every kind, from the smallest creatures to the largest. They investigate how animals move, feed and reproduce, and how their bodies are built. The study of animal life at the level of the cell connects zoology to [Cell Theory](cell-theory.md).

The naming and grouping of animals depends on [Taxonomy](taxonomy.md), the system developed by [Carl Linnaeus](carl-linnaeus.md), which allows the vast diversity of the animal kingdom to be studied in an orderly way.

## Zoology and the wider sciences

Zoology provided much of the evidence for the theory of [Evolution](evolution.md) and [Natural Selection](natural-selection.md) put forward by [Charles Darwin](charles-darwin.md), who studied animals closely during his travels. The study of inheritance in animals connects zoology to [Genetics](genetics.md).

The examination of animal remains from the past links zoology to [Palaeontology](palaeontology.md) and to the study of [Fossils](fossils.md), which reveal creatures of earlier ages.

Zoology includes many specialised fields, among them [Ornithology](ornithology.md), the study of birds. Together with [Botany](botany.md) it makes up the broad study of living things. Zoology has great value in medicine, agriculture and conservation, and it continues to deepen our understanding of the rich variety of animal life.
