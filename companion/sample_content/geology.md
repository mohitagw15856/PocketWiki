title: Geology
---

Geology is the science of the Earth, its rocks, its structure and its long history. It studies how the planet has changed over immense spans of time and how it continues to change today.

## Reading the rocks

Geologists examine layers of rock to work out the sequence of events in the Earth's past. The principle that lower layers are generally older than those above them allows the history of a region to be read like the pages of a book. Within these layers lie [Fossils](fossils.md), which help to date the rocks and to reveal past life.

Geology explains the formation of mountains, the action of rivers and glaciers, and the slow processes that shape the surface of the planet.

## Deep time and its influence

One of the great contributions of geology was the idea of deep time, the recognition that the Earth is extremely old. This understanding was essential for the theory of [Evolution](evolution.md), since gradual change requires vast periods.

The naturalist [Charles Darwin](charles-darwin.md) drew heavily on geology during his travels, and the explorer [Alexander von Humboldt](alexander-von-humboldt.md) combined geology with the study of plants and animals. The fossil discoveries of [Mary Anning](mary-anning.md) added greatly to geological knowledge.

Geology connects to [Palaeontology](palaeontology.md) and to the wider study of nature. It reveals a planet with a rich and dynamic past, and it remains vital for understanding the Earth we live on.
