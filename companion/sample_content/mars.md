title: Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from [The Sun](the-sun.md) and one of the nearest neighbours of the Earth in the [Solar System](the-solar-system.md). It is often called the red planet because of its reddish colour, which comes from iron-rich dust on its surface.

## A rocky world

Mars is a rocky planet, smaller than the Earth, with a thin atmosphere. Its surface bears great volcanoes, deep canyons and vast plains, and it has polar caps that grow and shrink with the seasons. There is strong evidence that liquid water once flowed there in the distant past.

Like the other [Planets](planets.md), Mars follows an elliptical orbit around the Sun, in keeping with the laws discovered by [Johannes Kepler](johannes-kepler.md).

## Observation and study

The motion of Mars across the sky puzzled early astronomers and played an important part in the story of [Heliocentrism](heliocentrism.md). It was by studying the orbit of Mars, using the precise observations of [Tycho Brahe](tycho-brahe.md), that Kepler found that planetary orbits are ellipses.

The [Telescope](telescope.md) revealed Mars as a distinct world with visible surface markings and polar caps. Its orbit is governed by the [Theory of Gravity](theory-of-gravity.md) of [Isaac Newton](isaac-newton.md).

Mars remains a major focus of [Astronomy](astronomy.md) and space exploration. As one of the most Earth-like of the planets, it holds particular interest for those studying the history of the Solar System and the possibility of past life.
