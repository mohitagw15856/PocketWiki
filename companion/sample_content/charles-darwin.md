title: Charles Darwin
---

Charles Darwin (1809 to 1882) was an English naturalist whose theory of [Evolution](evolution.md) by [Natural Selection](natural-selection.md) transformed the biological sciences. His observations during a long voyage aboard a survey ship gave him the evidence on which his ideas rested.

## The voyage and the theory

As a young man Darwin travelled around the world, studying [Zoology](zoology.md), [Botany](botany.md) and [Geology](geology.md). He was especially struck by the variety of species on remote islands and by the [Fossils](fossils.md) of extinct animals he found. Over many years he gathered evidence that living things change gradually across generations.

His central idea was that individuals better suited to their surroundings tend to survive and reproduce, so that helpful traits become more common over time.

## Publication and legacy

In 1859 Darwin published *On the Origin of Species*, which set out his argument in careful detail. His work drew on the study of [Taxonomy](taxonomy.md) begun by [Carl Linnaeus](carl-linnaeus.md) and was later strengthened by the [Genetics](genetics.md) of [Gregor Mendel](gregor-mendel.md).

Darwin was a cautious and thorough writer who collected observations for decades before publishing. His theory remains one of the central ideas of modern biology, uniting the study of living things under a single explanatory principle.
