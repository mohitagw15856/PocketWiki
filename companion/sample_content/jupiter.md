title: Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet in the [Solar System](the-solar-system.md), a giant world made mostly of gas. It is far bigger than all the other [Planets](planets.md) combined and shines brightly in the night sky.

## A giant planet

Jupiter has no solid surface like the Earth. It is a vast ball of gas, wrapped in colourful bands of clouds and marked by great storms, the most famous of which has raged for centuries. Around it circle many moons, the four largest of which are easily seen through a small [Telescope](telescope.md).

Jupiter follows an elliptical orbit around [The Sun](the-sun.md), obeying the laws described by [Johannes Kepler](johannes-kepler.md).

## A landmark in astronomy

The discovery of Jupiter's four large moons by [Galileo Galilei](galileo-galilei.md) was a turning point in the history of [Astronomy](astronomy.md). Here were bodies clearly circling another planet rather than the Earth, which supported the idea of [Heliocentrism](heliocentrism.md) and weakened the belief that everything moved around the Earth.

Jupiter's enormous mass gives it a powerful gravitational influence, in keeping with the [Theory of Gravity](theory-of-gravity.md) of [Isaac Newton](isaac-newton.md). It affects the orbits of nearby bodies, including some [Comets](comets.md).

Jupiter continues to be a major subject of study. Its size, its many moons and its dramatic atmosphere make it one of the most fascinating worlds in the Solar System, offering clues to how giant planets form and behave.
