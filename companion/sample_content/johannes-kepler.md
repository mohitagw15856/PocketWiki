title: Johannes Kepler

Johannes Kepler (1571 to 1630) was a German astronomer and mathematician who discovered the laws that describe how the [Planets](planets.md) move around [The Sun](the-sun.md). His work provided crucial support for [Heliocentrism](heliocentrism.md) and prepared the ground for later theories of motion.

## The laws of planetary motion

Working from the exceptionally precise observations of [Tycho Brahe](tycho-brahe.md), Kepler found three simple rules:

1. Each planet moves in an ellipse with the Sun at one focus.
2. A line joining a planet to the Sun sweeps out equal areas in equal times.
3. The square of a planet's orbital period is proportional to the cube of its average distance from the Sun.

These results replaced the old assumption that orbits must be perfect circles.

## Influence

Kepler's laws were later explained by [Isaac Newton](isaac-newton.md) through his [Theory of Gravity](theory-of-gravity.md), which showed why the [Solar System](the-solar-system.md) behaves as it does. Kepler also contributed to [Optics](optics.md), improving the understanding of how lenses form images in the [Telescope](telescope.md).

A devout and imaginative thinker, Kepler combined mathematical skill with painstaking calculation. His achievement in [Astronomy](astronomy.md) marked a turning point, moving the subject from geometric description towards physical explanation.
