title: Genetics
---

Genetics is the study of heredity, the way in which characteristics are passed from parents to offspring. It explains both the similarities within families and the variation that makes each individual unique.

## Foundations

The science of genetics began with the careful experiments of [Gregor Mendel](gregor-mendel.md), who crossed pea plants and found that traits are inherited in discrete units according to consistent rules. These units are now called genes.

Genes are carried within cells, linking genetics closely to [Cell Theory](cell-theory.md). Each gene holds instructions that influence how an organism grows and functions.

## Importance

Genetics provides the mechanism that underlies [Evolution](evolution.md) and [Natural Selection](natural-selection.md), showing how variation arises and how helpful traits can spread through a population. In this way it completes the picture begun by [Charles Darwin](charles-darwin.md).

The study of genetics draws on [Botany](botany.md) and [Zoology](zoology.md), since inheritance can be studied in plants and animals alike. It also connects to the classification of living things through [Taxonomy](taxonomy.md), because related species share much of their genetic inheritance.

Genetics has become one of the most active fields in biology, with applications in medicine, agriculture and conservation. From Mendel's pea plants to the detailed study of inheritance today, genetics continues to reveal how the instructions for life are stored, copied and passed on across the generations.
