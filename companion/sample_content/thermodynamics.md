title: Thermodynamics
---

Thermodynamics is the branch of [Physics](physics.md) that deals with heat, energy and the ways in which they change from one form to another. It describes how engines work and how energy flows through natural and artificial systems.

## The laws

Thermodynamics is built on a few general laws. One states that energy can be changed from one form to another but is never created or destroyed. Another describes how heat tends to spread out, so that systems move naturally towards a more disordered state and heat flows from warmer to cooler bodies.

These principles apply to everything from a cup of tea cooling in a room to the vast processes at work in [The Sun](the-sun.md).

## Importance

Thermodynamics grew from the practical study of steam engines and the desire to make them more efficient. It draws on ideas from [Chemistry](chemistry.md), since chemical reactions release or absorb energy, and it connects to [Electromagnetism](electromagnetism.md) through the many forms that energy can take.

The subject explains why some processes happen readily and others do not, and it sets limits on what any machine can achieve. Scientists such as [Michael Faraday](michael-faraday.md) contributed to the wider understanding of energy on which thermodynamics rests.

Thermodynamics is central to engineering, chemistry and astronomy. It provides a deep and general account of energy, and its laws are among the most reliable and far-reaching in all of science.
