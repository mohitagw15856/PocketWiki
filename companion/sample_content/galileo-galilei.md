title: Galileo Galilei
---

Galileo Galilei (1564 to 1642) was an Italian astronomer, physicist and engineer often described as a founder of modern observational science. His careful experiments and his use of the [Telescope](telescope.md) transformed the study of the heavens.

## Observations

Galileo improved the newly invented telescope and turned it towards the sky. He observed the mountains and craters of [The Moon](the-moon.md), the four large moons of [Jupiter](jupiter.md), the phases of Venus and the countless stars of the [Milky Way](milky-way.md). These findings supported [Heliocentrism](heliocentrism.md), the idea that the Earth and other [Planets](planets.md) move around [The Sun](the-sun.md).

## Physics and method

Beyond [Astronomy](astronomy.md), Galileo studied motion, showing that bodies fall with the same acceleration regardless of their weight. This work anticipated later ideas in [Physics](physics.md) and influenced [Isaac Newton](isaac-newton.md).

His support for the Sun-centred model brought him into conflict with the authorities of his day, and he spent his final years under house arrest. Despite this, his insistence on observation and measurement became a model for scientific enquiry. He is remembered as a patient experimenter who combined mathematics with direct observation, and his discoveries helped to establish that the universe could be understood through careful, repeatable study.
