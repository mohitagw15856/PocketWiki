title: Comets
---

Comets are small icy bodies that travel through the [Solar System](the-solar-system.md) on long orbits. When a comet approaches [The Sun](the-sun.md), it warms and releases gas and dust, forming a glowing head and a long tail.

## Nature and appearance

Far from the Sun a comet is a dark lump of ice, dust and rock. As it draws near, the ice turns to gas, creating a bright cloud around the solid core and a tail that streams away from the Sun. This striking appearance made comets famous throughout history, when they were often regarded as omens.

The paths of comets are governed by the [Theory of Gravity](theory-of-gravity.md) of [Isaac Newton](isaac-newton.md).

## Study of comets

The astronomer [Tycho Brahe](tycho-brahe.md) showed that comets travel far beyond [The Moon](the-moon.md), proving that they belong to the heavens rather than the Earth's atmosphere. Later, the study of their orbits allowed astronomers to predict the return of certain comets, the most famous being [Halley's Comet](halleys-comet.md).

Comets follow the same laws that guide the [Planets](planets.md), and their study forms part of [Astronomy](astronomy.md). Many comets return again and again on regular schedules, while others pass by only once.

Comets are thought to be ancient remnants from the formation of the [Solar System](the-solar-system.md). By studying them, scientists learn about the early materials from which the planets were made.
