title: Electromagnetism
---

Electromagnetism is the branch of [Physics](physics.md) that studies electric and magnetic forces and the close relationship between them. It is one of the fundamental interactions of nature and shapes much of the modern world.

## Electricity and magnetism united

For a long time electricity and magnetism were thought to be separate. Careful experiments showed that they are deeply linked. [Michael Faraday](michael-faraday.md) demonstrated that a changing magnetic field can create an electric current, while an electric current produces a magnetic field around it.

These discoveries led to the idea of the electromagnetic field, a region of influence that fills the space around charges and magnets.

## Light and technology

One of the great insights of electromagnetism is that light itself is an electromagnetic wave, a finding that connects the subject to [Optics](optics.md). This understanding built on the study of light begun by figures such as [Isaac Newton](isaac-newton.md).

Electromagnetism explains how electric motors, generators and countless devices work. The research of [Marie Curie](marie-curie.md) on radiation, and the arrangement of the [Periodic Table](periodic-table.md), both rest partly on electromagnetic forces between charged particles.

Electromagnetism unites electricity, magnetism and light within a single framework. It stands alongside the [Theory of Gravity](theory-of-gravity.md) as one of the pillars of classical physics, and it remains essential to science and technology today.
