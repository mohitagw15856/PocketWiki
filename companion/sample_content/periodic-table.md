title: Periodic Table
---

The periodic table is an arrangement of the chemical elements that reveals patterns in their properties. It is a central tool of [Chemistry](chemistry.md) and a concise summary of much of what is known about matter.

## Structure

The elements are ordered by the number of protons in their atoms, and arranged in rows and columns. Elements in the same column tend to share similar chemical behaviour, so the table groups together substances that react in comparable ways.

The layout allows chemists to predict how an element will behave and even to anticipate the existence of elements not yet discovered.

## Building the table

The modern table grew from the work of chemists who noticed repeating patterns in the properties of the elements. New elements have been added over time, including those isolated by [Marie Curie](marie-curie.md), whose research on radioactivity revealed polonium and radium.

The periodic table underpins much of [Physics](physics.md) as well as chemistry, since the arrangement reflects the structure of atoms themselves. It connects to the study of [Electromagnetism](electromagnetism.md), because chemical bonding depends on electric forces between charged particles.

Today the periodic table is one of the most recognisable images in science. It provides a framework that links the many elements into a single ordered system, and it continues to guide research in chemistry, materials science and the study of matter more broadly.
