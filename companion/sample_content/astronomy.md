Astronomy is the scientific study of objects and phenomena beyond the Earth, including [The Sun](the-sun.md), [The Moon](the-moon.md), the [Planets](planets.md), the stars and the wider universe. It is among the oldest of the sciences.

## History

Ancient peoples watched the sky to mark the seasons and to guide travel. A great change came with the model of [Heliocentrism](heliocentrism.md), proposed by [Nicolaus Copernicus](nicolaus-copernicus.md), which placed the Sun rather than the Earth at the centre of the [Solar System](the-solar-system.md).

The careful observations of [Tycho Brahe](tycho-brahe.md) allowed [Johannes Kepler](johannes-kepler.md) to discover the laws of planetary motion, and [Galileo Galilei](galileo-galilei.md) used the [Telescope](telescope.md) to reveal new features of the heavens.

## Modern astronomy

The [Theory of Gravity](theory-of-gravity.md) of [Isaac Newton](isaac-newton.md) explained the motions of the planets and [Comets](comets.md) and united the physics of the heavens with that of the Earth. Astronomy relies heavily on [Optics](optics.md) for its instruments and on [Physics](physics.md) for its theories.

Astronomers study the [Milky Way](milky-way.md) and the countless galaxies beyond it, seeking to understand the size, age and structure of the universe. Their work ranges from the nearest worlds of the Solar System to the most distant reaches of space.

Astronomy continues to expand our view of the cosmos, revealing a universe far larger and more remarkable than earlier generations could have imagined.
